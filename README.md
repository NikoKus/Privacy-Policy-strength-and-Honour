Privacy Policy – Strength and Honour: Quotes
Effective date: [13/11/2026]

This policy explains how the Android app Strength and Honour -Quotes, provided by [Bull and Banner Multiverse] handles information.

Summary
The App does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device
To work as you would expect, the App saves a few small items on your device only:

Your place in each chapter of quotes, and in the Ancient Stories book.
High scores for the quizzes, including the three-character name you type in. Please do not enter personal information in this field.
This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed if you clear the App’s storage or uninstall the App.

Internet use
The App’s content (quotes, biographies, battles, events, stories and explanations) is stored inside the App. [If you have not bundled the fonts: When you are online, the App may download its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means Google may receive your device’s IP address as part of that standard request. See Google’s privacy policy for details.]

Children
The App is general-audience educational content. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Third parties
The App contains no third-party advertising or analytics software.
